IRemember Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

Items stay on this device only. IRemember does not sync your items to cloud storage. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes your saved items, photos, and history. They cannot be recovered from IRemember.

1. Who we are

IRemember (“we”, “the app”) is a personal organization app that helps you remember where you stored everyday items. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and your choices.

2. Account information

When you create an account, we process your username, email address, and authentication credentials through Firebase Authentication and related Firebase/Google Cloud services. Account profile fields you choose to save may be stored with your account for sign-in and profile features. We use this data to authenticate you, secure your account, and provide account recovery features.

3. Item data (local only)

Items you add—including names, locations, categories, notes, photos, reminders, and history—are stored locally on your device. IRemember does not upload your item inventory to IRemember cloud storage. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes that local data.

4. Permissions

Depending on features you use, IRemember may request:

  • Microphone — for voice search
  • Notifications — for item reminders
  • Camera / photos — if you attach images to items
  • Internet — for account sign-in, password recovery, and news headlines

5. Third-party services

We use Google Firebase for accounts and related backend features; on-device / Google speech recognition for voice search; and external news RSS feeds when you open News. We do not sell your personal information.

6. Retention

  • Account data is retained while your account exists and as needed to provide the service.
  • Local items remain until you delete them or uninstall the app.
  • In-app History entries are automatically cleared after 30 days.

7. Uninstall and data loss

Uninstalling IRemember removes local app data on your device. IRemember cannot restore that local data after uninstall because it is not synced to IRemember servers.

8. Children’s privacy

IRemember is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer who published IRemember on Google Play, or open an issue on the project’s GitHub repository. Host this file (for example via GitHub Pages) and paste the public URL into the Google Play Console Privacy Policy field.
